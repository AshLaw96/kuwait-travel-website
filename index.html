Kuwait Escapes

Kuwait is a must-visit country in the Middle East. It might not be one of the most well-known travel destinations, but here's why it should be: beautiful architecture, stunning natural wonders, and a modern Arabic feel. Kuwait is more than a desert haven.

Lodgings

There is an abundance of places to stay in Kuwait whether it's luxury or family-friendly you're after. Here are just a few:

  • Waldorf Astoria Kuwait

    The Waldorf offers 200 lavish rooms, 5 restaurants, a luxurious spa, an outdoor pool, and an exclusive club. Perfect for fine dining and 5-star luxury.

  • Marriott Executive Apartments

    Studio apartments with one to three bedrooms, kitchenette, and dining area — a homely feel with 5-star comfort.

  • Hilton Kuwait Resort

    Comfortable rooms and private villas with pools — an ideal family getaway to explore Kuwait.

  • Sama Aljeyad

    An entire home in Abdali with its own football pitch and family activities — great for making unforgettable memories.

  • Flowery Holiday Suites

    Budget boutique accommodation with spacious rooms and a terrace view of Kuwait City's lights — quirky and affordable.

Activities

From thrilling adventures to peaceful explorations, Kuwait has something for everyone:

  • Kuwait Motor Town

    Off-roading and track racing — a paradise for motorheads.

  • Wibit Water Park

    Giant inflatable fun for all ages with zones for kids, adults, and those just relaxing at sea.

  • Marina World

    Over 150 shops along Kuwait's coastline — shop and admire the Gulf views.

  • The Grand Mosque

    Marvel at Islamic architecture and learn about Kuwaiti culture from expert guides.

Cuisines

Kuwait's culinary scene blends Arabian, Iranian, Indian, and Mediterranean flavors:

  • White Robata

    Award-winning Asian fusion dining that's as elegant as it is delicious.

  • Dar Hamad

    Authentic traditional Kuwaiti cuisine served in a beautiful setting.

  • Ovo

    Vegan and vegetarian twists on Middle Eastern dishes — healthy and satisfying.

  • The Cheesecake Factory

    Classic American diner meals followed by iconic cheesecake selections.

  • Crowne Plaza Rib Eye

    Family-friendly steakhouse with excellent service and mouthwatering meats.
