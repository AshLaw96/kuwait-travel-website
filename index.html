Activities

From thrilling adventures to peaceful explorations, Kuwait has something for everyone:

  • Kuwait Motor Town

    Off-roading and track racing — a paradise for motorheads.

  • Wibit Water Park

    Giant inflatable fun for all ages with zones for kids, adults, and those just relaxing at sea.

  • Marina World

    Over 150 shops along Kuwait's coastline — shop and admire the Gulf views.

  • The Grand Mosque

    Marvel at Islamic architecture and learn about Kuwaiti culture from expert guides.

Cuisines

Kuwait's culinary scene blends Arabian, Iranian, Indian, and Mediterranean flavors:

  • White Robata

    Award-winning Asian fusion dining that's as elegant as it is delicious.

  • Dar Hamad

    Authentic traditional Kuwaiti cuisine served in a beautiful setting.

  • Ovo

    Vegan and vegetarian twists on Middle Eastern dishes — healthy and satisfying.

  • The Cheesecake Factory

    Classic American diner meals followed by iconic cheesecake selections.

  • Crowne Plaza Rib Eye

    Family-friendly steakhouse with excellent service and mouthwatering meats.
